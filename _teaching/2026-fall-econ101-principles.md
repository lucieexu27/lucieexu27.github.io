Instructor of record, Fall 2026.

My goal in Principles of Microeconomics is to help students develop an intuitive understanding of how economists approach questions about markets, incentives, and resource allocation. I build core models step-by-step and emphasize their application to real-world questions, encouraging students to move beyond memorizing economic concepts to using them as tools for analyzing unfamiliar problems. In particular, I discuss applications of microeconomics in the last few lectures, including current research topics.
